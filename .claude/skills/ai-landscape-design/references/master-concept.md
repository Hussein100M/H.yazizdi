# Master Landscape Concept from a single image

Use this when the user wants the thinking, not just one edited picture — "ماستر لاندسكيب", a concept, a zoning study, a landscape strategy, or slides for a presentation. The deliverable is a short written concept plus the image passes that illustrate it.

The point of the concept is that everything downstream — every prompt, every pass, every added bench — comes from one idea instead of from taste in the moment. That is the whole difference between AI decoration and AI design.

## Deliverable structure

Write it in Arabic (the user's language) with the English design terms kept as-is, in this order:

1. **قراءة الموقع** — 4–6 bullets from Step 1 of the skill: project type, view, climate, scale ruler, sun direction, existing surfaces and circulation, what is missing today.
2. **الفكرة التصميمية (Concept Statement)** — two or three sentences naming the organising idea and the experience it creates. Not "green and beautiful"; something with a structure behind it: an oasis sequence from hot arrival to cool shaded core; a green spine linking the blocks; a bosque frame holding an open civic room; a dune-and-wadi language following the site's own geometry.
3. **جدول المناطق (Zone Table)** — one row per zone:

   | الزون | الدور | العناصر الرئيسية | نسبة تقريبية |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Entry Forecourt | استقبال وتأطير المدخل | نخيل متقابل، مسطح منسق، إضاءة أرضية | 10% |

4. **استراتيجية الزراعة (Planting Strategy)** — the palette by layer with mature sizes, and where each layer concentrates. Say what the ground is where there is no planting.
5. **الحركة والمسارات (Circulation)** — pedestrian spine, secondary paths, vehicle and service routes kept clear, arrival sequence, the shaded route.
6. **برنامج العناصر (Furniture & Urban Elements)** — counts by zone: seating, shade structures, lighting, play, water, bins, signage.
7. **الخطة البصرية (Image Passes)** — the sequence of prompts that will produce the visuals, each one a pass as defined in the skill. Deliver Pass 1 in full; list the others as one line each.
8. **ملاحظات التنفيذ** — assumptions made, what could not be read from the image, what to confirm on site (levels, irrigation, soil depth over slabs, maintenance intensity).

## Percentages that keep a concept honest

Rough green-to-hard balance, useful when the user asks for numbers:

- Public park: 60–75% soft, 25–40% hard
- Residential compound: 40–55% soft
- Hotel / resort: 45–60% soft, with water counted separately
- Plaza: 20–35% soft — a plaza that goes green stops being a plaza
- Campus: 40–50% soft, with wide hard spines
- Villa: 30–50% soft, driven by how the family uses the plot
- Commercial: 20–35% soft, concentrated at entries and parking islands

## Presentation framing

When the concept is going into slides, the title the user works with is:

> **AI Landscape Design — من تحليل المشهد إلى Master Landscape**

A slide sequence that carries the story: the original image → site reading with zones marked → concept statement → zone table → planting layers → furniture programme → the final visual (and a night shot if one was produced). Keep one idea per slide, and put the before/after pair on the last slide, side by side, because that is the slide people remember.

If the user asks for the actual deck file, hand the content to the pptx skill rather than describing slides here.
