# Worked examples

Two complete outputs in the skill's format. Read one when you want to see the density and tone the prompt should have — not to copy the species, which come from the site.

## Example 1 — Private villa, arid Gulf, eye-level exterior

**تحليل المشهد:** فيلا سكنية من طابقين، لقطة أرضية أمامية؛ مناخ خليجي جاف؛ المقياس: ارتفاع الدور ≈ 3.2 م؛ الشمس من اليسار والظلال قصيرة (قرب الظهيرة)؛ الأرض رملية مسوّاة مع ممر أسفلت للسيارة.

**مناطق الـ Landscape:** Entry Forecourt: تأطير المدخل / Boundary Strip: تليين السور وحجب الجيران / Side Terrace: جلسة مظللة / Driveway Edge: تظليل موقف السيارة.

**الصور المطلوبة:** Image A = صورة الفيلا الحالية.

```
Use Image A as the base. Keep the camera position, lens, framing, perspective and aspect ratio exactly as they are, and keep the villa itself completely unchanged: massing, two storeys, facade material and colour, every window and door, the entrance canopy, the parapet and the boundary wall stay exactly as in Image A. Keep the existing asphalt driveway, its width and its alignment, and keep the midday sun coming from the left with the same short shadows and the same sky. The floor-to-floor height of the villa is about 3.2 m — size all planting against it.

Design the landscape of the plot. In the entry forecourt on either side of the gate, plant two matched date palms with 5 m clear trunks, one to each side, and below them a continuous 0.8 m bed of clipped Carissa hedge with a mat of Lantana ground cover at its front. Along the boundary strip against the wall, run a continuous planting bed 1.2 m deep with Oleander at 2.5 m for screening behind, low Ixora at 0.8 m in drifts of five in front, and the bed surface finished in buff gravel. Set one Sidr tree 8 m tall with a broad canopy on the right side of the plot, standing clear of the facade so the elevation stays fully visible, and let it cast a real shadow to the right in the existing sun direction. At the driveway edge, add two Ghaf trees 8 m tall spaced 8 m apart so their canopies shade the parked car. Keep a small rectangular lawn of about 6 by 4 m on the side terrace only, with a clean cut edge against buff stone paving; everywhere else the ground becomes compacted decomposed granite in a warm sand tone with a few natural boulders.

PRESERVE: the villa and every part of its facade, the entrance and gate, the boundary wall, the asphalt driveway clear of any planting, the neighbouring buildings, the street, the sky, the sun direction and the length and direction of all existing shadows.

ADD: 2 date palms, 1 Sidr tree, 2 Ghaf trees, the boundary screening bed, the forecourt hedge and ground cover, one small lawn, decomposed granite ground finish, natural boulders.

The result must read as the same villa from the same viewpoint at the same hour, with only the landscape changed. New trees and palms cast shadows in the same direction as the existing ones. Photorealistic, same resolution and aspect ratio, no text, logos or watermarks.
```

**ملاحظات:**
- تأكد أول شيء من مقياس الأشجار: تاج شجرة السدر لازم يوصل تقريبًا لمستوى الدور الثاني، لا يغطي الفيلا.
- أكثر خطأ متوقع هنا: زراعة فوق ممر السيارة — إذا صار، كرر جملة المنع بصياغة "the driveway remains clear asphalt".
- افترضت مناخ خليجي جاف؛ لو الموقع ساحلي أعطني المدينة وأبدّل الباليت.
- Pass 2 لاحقًا: برجولة على جلسة الحديقة الجانبية + إضاءة أرضية.

## Example 2 — Public park, aerial view

**تحليل المشهد:** أرض عامة مسوّاة بمحيط شوارع، لقطة جوية؛ مناخ جاف؛ المقياس: عرض الشارع المجاور ≈ 12 م؛ الشمس من أعلى اليسار؛ لا توجد زراعة قائمة.

**مناطق الـ Landscape:** Main Entry Plaza: استقبال وتجمع / Shaded Loop Promenade: مسار مشي مظلل / Central Event Lawn: تجمعات ومناسبات / Picnic Groves: جلسات عائلية بالظل / Children's Play: لعب آمن مظلل / Perimeter Buffer: عزل عن الشارع.

**الصور المطلوبة:** Image A = اللقطة الجوية للموقع.

```
Use Image A as the base. Keep the camera position, altitude, framing, perspective and aspect ratio exactly as they are, and keep the surrounding streets, buildings, plot boundaries, sun direction and sky unchanged. The adjacent street is about 12 m wide — size everything against it.

Design this site as a public park. At the main entry on the street side, create a paved forecourt about 20 by 15 m in large buff stone paving, framed by two groups of five Ghaf trees 9 m tall. From the forecourt, run a 4 m wide looping promenade of pale stone paving around the site, lined on both sides with Sidr trees 8 m tall spaced 8 m apart so the whole walk is shaded, with benches 1.8 m long in pairs every 40 m and 1 m bollard lights every 8 m. In the centre, place an open lawn roughly 45 by 30 m with a clean curved edge for events. Between the lawn and the promenade, create three picnic groves, each a cluster of seven trees over decomposed granite ground with four shaded picnic tables and a pergola 4 by 6 m. In the northern corner, set a children's play area of about 20 by 20 m on blue and yellow rubber safety surface with play structures 3 m high, enclosed by a low shrub bed, with two shaded benches facing it. Around the whole perimeter, run a 5 m deep buffer of massed Oleander at 2.5 m behind drifts of Lantana ground cover. Ground that is not paved, lawn or bed becomes decomposed granite in a warm sand tone.

PRESERVE: the site boundary and its shape, the surrounding streets, junctions and pavements exactly as they are, the neighbouring buildings and their roofs, the sky, the sun direction and all existing shadow directions.

ADD: entry forecourt, shaded loop promenade with benches and bollard lights, central event lawn, three picnic groves with pergolas and tables, children's play area, perimeter planting buffer, decomposed granite ground finish.

The result must read as a real designed park photographed from the same altitude and viewpoint under the same light, with tree canopies casting shadows in the existing sun direction and the street network untouched. Photorealistic, same resolution and aspect ratio, no text, logos or watermarks.
```

**ملاحظات:**
- راجع أول شيء نسبة الأشجار للشارع: تاج الشجرة لازم يكون حوالي نصف عرض الشارع، لا أكبر.
- الخطأ المتوقع في اللقطات الجوية: تغيير شبكة الشوارع أو حواف الأرض — إذا صار، قصّر وصف المناطق وقسّمه على مرحلتين.
- Pass 2: نافورة ومسطح مائي في محور اللقطة + كشك خدمات؛ Pass 3: لقطة ليلية بالإضاءة.
