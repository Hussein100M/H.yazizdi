# Furniture vocabulary

Read this when describing a reference piece, writing a suggestion, or sizing a piece against the room. Image models rebuild what is named precisely and reinvent what is named loosely: "a sofa" gets redesigned, "a low three-seat sofa with a deep plinth base, two loose seat cushions and sloped track arms" survives.

## 1 Describing a piece (order)

Write the design in this order, and only what the image shows (✓); a ≈ detail is written by its look, a ? detail is left out:

1. **Type and silhouette** — low and deep / upright / curved / boxy / floating on legs / sitting on a plinth.
2. **Proportions** — length against depth, back height against arm height, seat thickness.
3. **Base** — legs (count, shape, material, splay) or plinth / sled / pedestal / trestle.
4. **Body parts** — arms, back, cushions (count, loose or fixed), headboard, top, drawers, shade.
5. **Surface** — upholstery or material, colour, finish (matte, satin, gloss), texture.
6. **Details** — piping, welt, channel stitching, tufting, buttons, fluting, reeding, edge profile, handles, visible joints.

## 2 Parts by type

- **Sofa / sectional** — frame; seat cushions (one bench cushion or two or three loose); back cushions (fixed, loose, scatter-back); arms: track (square), sloped, rolled, flared, shelter (as high as the back), armless; base: plinth, recessed plinth (floating look), tapered legs, block feet, sled; chaise end, corner module, ottoman; skirt; piping; channel tufting; deep buttoning.
- **Armchair / lounge chair** — wingback, barrel (curved back wrapping into the arms), slipper (armless, low), club, shell or tub, lounge with ottoman, cantilever frame; open frame (timber or metal) with sling or loose cushions.
- **Dining / side chair** — seat (upholstered pad, woven cord, rush, cane, solid timber), backrest (spindles, ladder, solid, upholstered), front and back legs, stretchers, arms or armless.
- **Table** — top (shape, thickness, edge profile: square, bevelled, bullnose, knife edge), base (four legs, pedestal, twin pedestals, trestle, sled, plinth), apron; nesting set; coffee table with a lower shelf.
- **Bed** — headboard (upholstered, panelled, channel-tufted, wingback, timber slats), bed frame or platform, footboard, side rails, bedding (duvet, throw, pillows); bedside tables.
- **Storage** — sideboard / credenza / console / TV unit / bookcase; fronts: flat, fluted, reeded glass, cane, slatted; handles (pulls, knobs, recessed grip, push-to-open); base or legs; top material.
- **Lighting** — pendant (drum, globe, dome, linear, cluster), chandelier (tiers, arms, crystals), floor lamp (arc, tripod, column, torchiere), table lamp (base and shade), wall sconce; shade material (linen, paper, opal glass, rattan, metal) and how it glows.
- **Soft furnishings** — rug (shape, pile: flat-weave, low, high, shag; border; pattern scale), curtains (sheer or blackout, pleats, track hidden or visible, length to floor), cushions and throws.

## 4 Materials by look

Write a material by its look and finish, the way a buyer would recognise it in a photo:

- **Bouclé** — dense small loops, soft matte, catches light on the loop tops.
- **Velvet** — short dense pile, sheen that changes with direction, darker in the folds.
- **Linen / linen blend** — visible slub weave, soft matte, gentle relaxed creases.
- **Leather** — aniline (natural grain, slight colour variation, soft creases), semi-aniline or corrected (more even, light sheen); cognac, tan, black, chocolate.
- **Cane / rattan / woven cord** — open hexagonal cane mesh, wrapped rattan poles, tight woven paper cord.
- **Timber** — oak (straight or flat-sawn grain; natural, white-oiled, smoked), walnut (dark warm brown, flowing grain), ash, teak, burl veneer (swirling figure); oiled, lacquered or matte.
- **Stone** — travertine (cream, small pores and voids, honed), Calacatta or Carrara marble (white with grey or gold veining), Nero Marquina (black with white veins), terrazzo (chips in a ground); honed or polished.
- **Metal** — brushed brass, polished chrome, black or bronze powder-coated steel, satin stainless; thin profiles with crisp edges.
- **Glass** — clear, smoked (grey tint), bronze-tinted, fluted or reeded, frosted.
- **Lacquer / paint** — high-gloss or satin lacquer, matte painted MDF.
