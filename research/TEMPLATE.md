# Reference analysis template

One block per image, appended to `research/log/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
Evidence marks, as in clay-rerender: ✓ seen · ≈ likely · ? cannot tell. A ≈ or ? is never written as a fact.
Ignore Instagram overlays (icons, captions, watermarks) — read only the picture.

```markdown

- **Kind:** real photo | CG render | AI image (≈ if unsure) · **Project:** villa (style …) | residential tower | office / commercial | hospitality | interior (room …) | masterplan / aerial | landscape | other (…) · **View:** exterior eye level | low angle | aerial | interior
- **Quality gate:** keep | weak — <why> (AI artefacts, heavy filters, overlays, low resolution: weak refs are logged but not tallied)

- Shadows: strength (≈ stops between sun and shade), pattern (crisp, dappled …), where they fall

- Air: haze, depth, distance falloff
- Artificial lighting: each fixture → the light it throws on the surfaces around it · ≈ K · its state against the daylight
- People: count · placement · pose · dress · scale · facing

- Surfaces, largest to smallest: element → real material · colour · finish · joints
- Reflections: glass (what it reflects, glancing vs head-on), water, polished stone and metal
- Plants & landscape: kinds, arrangement, maturity, scale against the building
- Grade, in words: highlights · shadows · saturation · contrast · warmth
```
