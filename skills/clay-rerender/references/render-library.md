## R — Luxury score (الفخامة /10), rubric v0

Used in the weekly A/B review next to مطابقة التصميم and الواقعية. v0 is a starting point; it is rewritten from what the owner's picks show.

| Check | 2 — strong | 1 — partly | 0 — missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | Directional light that models the forms and shows the materials' texture | Correct but flat in places | Flat, even CG light |
| Materials | Stone, metal and timber read as real and costly: variation, sheen, joints | Mostly real, one surface plastic or chalky | Plastic, chalky or flat-painted |
| Calm | Uncluttered; people and objects few and placed with intent | Slightly busy | Crowded or random |
| Landscape | Mature, groomed and in scale with the building | Thin or young in places | Sparse, cloned or pasted |
| Finish | Clean warm whites, controlled colour, open shadows | Slightly off (cast, crushed blacks) | Oversaturated or muddy |

Total out of 10. A result that loses design fidelity scores 0 here as well.
